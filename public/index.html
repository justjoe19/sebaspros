Sebas Pros Lawn Care specializes in professional and affordable leaf cleanups in South Bend, Indiana, and surrounding areas. FAQ

Common questions

Are estimates free?

Yes. Estimates are always free. Call or text 574-360-5040, or send the form on this page.

What services does Sebas Pros offer?

Leaf cleanups, leaf blowing and piling to the curb, leaf removal and hauling, full-service fall cleanups, lawn mowing, weed trimming, lawn edging, bush trimming and general yard cleanups.

How much do lawn mowing and leaf cleanup cost?

Starting prices. Lawn Mowing: small yards $40+, medium yards $50+, large yards $65+. Leaf Blowing: small yards $60+, medium yards $100+, large yards $150+, very large yards $200–$500+. Leaf Removal: all yard sizes $80+. Estimates are free.

Do you haul leaves away, or only pile them at the curb?

Both. Choose curbside piles for municipal pickup, or have the leaves removed and hauled away.

What areas do you serve?

South Bend, Indiana, and surrounding areas across Michiana, including Mishawaka, Granger, Osceola, Elkhart, Goshen and nearby towns in Northern Indiana, plus Niles, Buchanan, Edwardsburg and other Southwest Michigan communities. If you don't see your town, just ask.
